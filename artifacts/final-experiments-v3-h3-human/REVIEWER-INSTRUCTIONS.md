# Reviewer instructions

You will evaluate 40 explanations generated under different information
presentation conditions. Evaluate explanation quality only; do not try to infer
an item's experimental condition.

Use only the explanation and supporting context supplied in your CSV. Do not
use external tools or LLMs to score items. Do not judge whether the root cause
itself is correct: the study evaluates explanation quality for a fixed RCA
result.

For every row, enter integers from 1 through 5 for utility, clarity, perceived
confidence, traceability, and uncertainty adequacy, following the frozen rubric.
Do not leave a rating blank and do not alter the item or reviewer identifiers.
