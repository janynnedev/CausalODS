# H3 human-evaluation rubric v1

Enter one integer—1, 2, 3, 4, or 5—for every dimension. Scores 2 and 4 are the
intermediate positions between their adjacent anchors. Judge only the supplied
explanation and supporting context. Do not judge whether the fixed root-cause
result is correct.

## Utility

- 1 — Would not help an engineer investigate or act.
- 3 — Partially useful but requires substantial additional work.
- 5 — Directly useful for investigation or action.

## Clarity

- 1 — Confusing or difficult to understand.
- 3 — Understandable with some effort.
- 5 — Clear, concise, and easy to understand.

## Perceived confidence

This means how well-supported or credible the explanation appears from the
supplied context—not how confident the evaluator feels personally.

- 1 — Appears weakly supported.
- 3 — Moderately supported.
- 5 — Appears strongly supported.

## Traceability

- 1 — Claims cannot be related to the supplied context or evidence.
- 3 — Some claims are traceable.
- 5 — Claims are readily traceable to supplied context or evidence.

## Uncertainty adequacy

- 1 — Certainty or uncertainty is misleading or inappropriate.
- 3 — Partially adequate.
- 5 — Uncertainty is communicated appropriately given the evidence.
